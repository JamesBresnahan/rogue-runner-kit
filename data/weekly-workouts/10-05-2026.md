min 73). `workout_feel` **75 ("Good")**, `workout_rpe` **30 (RPE
3/10)** — an easy-feeling day. Planned: 8mi total — easy first half,
5x(2' @ MP 6:29/mi / 2' easy @ EZ Runs 6:30–7:29/mi) second half, no
separate cool-down.

**Structural note:** the open-ended ("first half") warm-up ran to
6.20mi — notably longer than the roughly 4mi a clean 8mi-total split
would suggest — likely reflecting a hillier-than-usual route choice
(184m of climb packed into the warm-up's first 2.9mi alone). As a
result, only **4 of the 5 planned MP reps** were completed before
total distance hit 8.40mi, and there was **no separate cool-down** —
the activity ends immediately after the 4th recovery jog (which itself
was cut 2 seconds short, 118s vs. the planned 120s). Worth knowing for
next time if a full 5-rep set matters more than hitting a clean
mid-8mi-ish total.

**Warm-up (6.20mi, 51:44):** averaged 8:20/mi vs. EZ Runs target
6:30-7:29/mi — slower than target, consistent with hilly terrain and
the standing convention that warm-ups aren't judged against the
target. Avg HR 127 (max 146).
