Running injury/pain reports are logged here automatically, in a single
ever-growing `injury-log.md`, once you mention pain, soreness, or an
injury and confirm saving, via the `track-injury` skill. Each issue is
tracked as its own episode that can span multiple dated check-ins over
time (better/worse/same/gone), with the workout(s) around each check-in
noted — including the day(s) before, since running pain often takes 24-48
hours to show up. Correlation against past entries only kicks in once
there's enough tracking history across multiple workouts — a single
report is just logged, not analyzed for causes yet.
