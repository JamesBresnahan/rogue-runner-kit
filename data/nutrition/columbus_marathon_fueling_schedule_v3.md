## Pre-Race Preparation

* **2.5 Hours Before Gun:** 1 Cup of Coffee (~95mg caffeine) + Pre-race Breakfast (below)
* **30 Minutes Before Gun:** 1 Huma Plus Gel (24g carbs, 25mg caffeine)

### Pre-Race Breakfast (2.5 hrs out)
**Goal:** ~2 g/kg carbs ≈ **145g**

| Item | Amount | Carbs (approx.) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Sourdough toast | 2 slices | 35–50g |
| Banana | 1 medium | 27g |
| Honey (drizzled on toast) | 1 tbsp | 17g |
| Almond butter | Thin layer, ~1 tbsp | 3g |
| **Base total** | | **~85–100g (1.2–1.4 g/kg)** |

**To close the gap to ~145g, add one or more:**
* Increase honey to 2–3 tbsp (+17–34g)
* 12–16 oz orange juice or sports drink (+35–45g)
* A third slice of sourdough (+18–25g)

**Notes:**
* Keep the almond butter thin. Fat slows gastric emptying, and a heavy spread is the one item that could still be sitting in your stomach at the gun.
* Eat this exact breakfast, at the same 2.5-hour lead time, before your last long workout.

## Logistical & Packing Execution

### 1. Inventory Checklist
* **Huma Plus (Regular):** 1 Packet (Pre-race Top-Off)
* **Huma Ultra:** 3 Pouches (Race Fueling)
* **Clif Bloks (Black Cherry):** 10 individual chews total. A packet holds 6 chews, so buy **2 packets** (12 chews) and count out exactly 10. Pre-pack them in drop order: **2 / 3 / 3 / 2**. Separate Ziploc bags or a marked sleeve make it easy to grab the right count at race pace.

### 2. The Plain Water Protocol
For all three **Huma Ultra** drops (Miles 6.0, 11.5, and 18.8), take strictly **plain water**. Don't combine these high-carb purees with the course Gatorade Endurance mix. Stacking the extra carbs and osmolality raises the risk of slowed gastric emptying and sloshing.

### 3. Rehearse the Actual Intake Rate
All 200g goes in between 0:20 and 2:13, so your gut sees roughly **105g/hour** during that window, which is higher than the 71g/hr race average. Run this exact schedule, including the 3-Blok drops, on at least one long run at or near marathon pace before race day.

### 4. Verify Water Stops
Confirm the Mile 3, 6 (Bexley), 9.5, 11–12, 15.5 (Lane Ave), 17, 18.8 (Grandview), and 20.7 stops against this year's official course map. Shift any fuel drop to the nearest confirmed table if one has moved.

### 5. Wind-Down After Mile 20.7
The last solid fuel goes in at 2:13:30. That leaves about 35 minutes for it to clear the stomach, so it's still useful late in the race. After that, stick to water and optional mouth rinses. Trust the plan, execute the drops cleanly, and empty the tank to the line.
