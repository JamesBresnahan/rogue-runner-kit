## Energy balance context (Garmin, same day)

- Total: 3,388 kcal (active 1,726 + BMR 1,662). BMR is Garmin's estimate
  of resting energy needs (organ function, breathing, circulation — no
  movement) from profile data; active is Garmin's estimate of
  calories burned through movement/exercise on top of that.
- The run itself accounted for 1,414 kcal of the active total; the
  remaining ~312 kcal is Garmin's background wrist-based estimate for
  everything else that day, including the 25-min strength session plus
  general daily movement (25,080 steps).
- **Likely undercounted:** the strength session wasn't started as a
  tracked activity (only the run appears in `get_activities_fordate`),
  so its calories come from Garmin's passive HR/accelerometer
  algorithm rather than dedicated activity tracking. That passive
  algorithm is calibrated for rhythmic cardio and is known to
  undercount resistance training, where movement is set/rest-based
  and load-bearing rather than continuous. A 25-min leg session on its
  own is more typically 150-250 kcal — likely more than its share of
  the 312 kcal blended in with a full day's incidental steps. Actual
  total expenditure was probably somewhat higher than the reported
  3,388 kcal.
- This is a full-day final figure (`lastSyncTimestampGMT` after
  midnight), not a partial-day snapshot.
